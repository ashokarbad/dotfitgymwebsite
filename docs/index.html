About Dotfit

More than a gym.
A community.
A lifestyle.

Dotfit Fitness is a complete fitness destination in Baner, Pune. Strength and conditioning on the floor, coached classes through the day, sports performance work, nutrition that fits an Indian kitchen — and a community that trains together well beyond the gym, from the Dotfit Premier League to weekend treks.

  • Structured, goal-based programming
  • Trainer-supervised sessions
  • Nutrition built into the plan
  • Activities, sport and events all year
  • Every fitness level welcome
  • Online and home training options
